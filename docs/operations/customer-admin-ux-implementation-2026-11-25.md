## Whole-branch review fixes

Checkout now distinguishes a lost payment response from an explicit decline. A dropped connection, stalled headers or body, or unreadable successful reply goes to status recovery for the prepared order. The shopper can check again without submitting another charge. A pre-charge preparation or permit timeout leaves the draft and current attempt available. Late completion cannot advance an expired operation. Wallet and guest verification initialization have local retry controls. Native approval remains with the provider.

Transactions, Shipping and Pickups retain a confirmed page during pagination within the authorized queue. Loading and error feedback identify the displayed page. Keyboard controls remain mounted, stale rows cannot issue fulfillment commands and access denial clears protected results. Homepage merchandise photos retain geometry during load and failure. The initiating link shows navigation feedback, including hard-document checkout entry, and a modified staff link keeps the original drawer open.

The local ownership runner protects partial setup before creating the runtime. Two setup-failure regressions stop during source copy and after junction creation, verify cleanup and leave the dependency target intact. Neither probe creates a user or starts a backend. The verification table below records its original evidence dates and revisions. [Final-review coverage](customer-admin-ux-coverage-2026-11-25.md#checkout-and-navigation-recovery-after-final-review)

## Residual review fixes

A confirmed 401 or 403 from an order list or any queue count now closes open Shipping and Pickups order dialogs. A delayed label action checks the current queue again before requesting rates or purchasing a label. Purchases already submitted still record their final or unresolved outcome for operator recovery. A transient count failure preserves the authorized item details. Shipping origin configuration is scoped to the current staff owner independently of order-list readiness. Its editor opens and saves during a list 503 or held refresh; denial or staff replacement clears the draft and blocks stale saves. A replacement owner must first load its own configuration.

Stopping a native cart-to-checkout request can leave the original document visible. Its loading announcement now clears after 15 seconds or on page restoration. For guest checkout with verification unavailable, the payment panel displays the reason above Pay and associates it with the disabled payment controls. The security check still gates payment.

## Remaining release gates

- Exercise staging with its real host, CSP, cookies, rate limits, recovery delivery and provider configuration. Verify guest and signed-in shipping/pickup, cancellation, delayed resolution and receipt access against actual provider outcomes.
- Exercise iOS Safari and Android devices with the virtual keyboard, touch gestures, camera, clipboard permission, reduced motion, zoom and long modal content. Check VoiceOver, TalkBack and desktop screen-reader announcements, reading order and landmark navigation.
- Exercise wallet eligibility, browser payment sheets, issuer challenges and SDK recovery on supported devices. Charging, refunding, label purchases, fulfillment, outbound messages and live analytics were outside local acceptance.
