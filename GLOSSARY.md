# Sole Sneakers

Sole Sneakers operates one retail store. Independent seller accounts and multiple
tenant businesses are outside this store's scope; a separate platform is being
built for that purpose.

## Language

**Store**:
Sole Sneakers, the single retail business that operates this catalog, inventory,
and customer orders.
_Avoid_: Tenant or separate seller account when referring to the Store.

**Customer**:
A person shopping with the Store, either as a guest or through a customer account.

**Staff**:
A person authorized to manage the Store and its operations.
